Customer Retention & Loyalty Analytics Dashboard

Python SQL Power BI

Analyzed over 32 million Instacart order records using Python for data preparation and advanced SQL queries, before developing a comprehensive 3-page interactive Power BI dashboard tracking purchase behavior, customer retention patterns, and loyalty metrics.

The company needed to model repeat purchasing behavior over 32M records to isolate user purchasing frequency, identify products driving repeat business, and design a loyalty tier system (Active vs. Occasional cohorts) to support targeted customer retention campaigns.
  • Discovered that order reorder frequency peaks sharply at Day 7 and Day 30, suggesting clear weekly and monthly shopping habits.
  • Identified that the "Loyal" tier (accounting for 15% of the user base) contributes over 58% of aggregate order volume.
  • Highlighted top 10 reordered items in fresh produce and dairy, recommending placement optimization on the mobile application layout.

Cloud Kitchen Profitability Analytics Dashboard

SQL Tableau

Designed an interactive 2-page Tableau dashboard to evaluate multi-brand cloud kitchen sales, brand performance, margins, menu insights, and operational KPIs. Powered by a SQL relational model using 80+ target business queries.

The operator wanted to measure brand-level profitability across multiple kitchens, analyze channel margins (Direct Orders vs. Aggregators like Zomato/Swiggy), optimize product margins, and reduce food waste on underperforming menu items.
  • Discovered that direct channel orders generate 15% higher profit margins than aggregator platforms due to high commission fees.
  • Isolated 4 underperforming menu items that suffered from high ingredient costs and low volumes, suggesting a menu consolidation.
  • Identified a 20% variance in operational efficiency between cooking stations, facilitating shifts in staff allocations.

Credit Risk and Loan Analytics Dashboard

Power BI SQL DAX

Developed a 2-page risk analytics dashboard using Prosper loan datasets. Highlights borrower credit quality, debt-to-income (DTI) trends, default risk factors, and loan segments using custom DAX measures and a star schema schema.

The credit department required an interactive tracking tool to analyze default patterns, evaluate loans based on borrower credit grades, assess high bankcard utilization, and adjust credit limits to mitigate high-risk exposures.
  • Discovered that borrowers with DTI ratios above 35% default at a 2.3x higher rate compared to DTI levels below 20%.
  • Determined that a bankcard utilization rate above 80% correlates with a significant rise in late payments (Past Due 31-90 days).
  • Segmented the portfolio by rating, proving that C-graded loans produced the highest net interest margins despite elevated risk.

  • Discovered that European sales margins lagged behind North American margins by 8.2% due to localized shipping costs.
